Market Glossary (seed document)

- Market capitalization: total value of a company's outstanding shares.
- P/E ratio: share price divided by earnings per share.
- Volatility: the degree of variation in a trading price over time.
- Sentiment: the overall attitude of investors toward a security or market,
  often inferred from news flow and price action.
- Drawdown: the peak-to-trough decline during a specific period for an investment.
